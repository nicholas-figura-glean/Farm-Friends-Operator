Why this game is an epistemic time capsule

  • Everything here scales. The real farm ultimately did too. A short run-46 cohort falsely looked capped, froze growth for 246 runs, and was later superseded by the full leaderboard fit.
  • Wheat grows here. Real wheat, corn and pumpkin stayed at 0% grown for 27 minutes in one historical server regime. That negative result is scoped and overdue for revalidation.
  • Ceiling Lift I and II are named after the ceiling we mistakenly promoted from a hypothesis into permanent policy.
  • Produce sells at 2 coins and a manager costs 20x a producer's base, because the real prices are still worth learning.
  • The achievements preserve the wrong turns. Wool, At Last and Milk, At Last remember species that were negligible in collected output without claiming they can never score.
