Why this game is an epistemic time capsule

  • This game is designed to keep scaling. For the real farm, the current leaderboard evidence supports output increasing with herd size over the observed range; it does not prove unlimited scaling. That evidence superseded a short run-46 collection cohort that appeared capped and froze growth for 246 runs.
  • Wheat grows here. Real wheat, corn and pumpkin stayed at 0% grown for 27 minutes in one historical server regime. That negative result is scoped and overdue for revalidation.
  • Ceiling Lift I and II are named after the run-46 collection cap we mistakenly promoted from a hypothesis into permanent policy.
  • The economy is deliberately abstract. Each game produce unit converts to 2 coins and managers cost 20x a producer's base. Those values are inspired by selected real-farm prices, not a model of its full economy.
  • The achievements preserve the wrong turns. Wool, At Last and Milk, At Last remember species that were negligible in the run-50 collection cohort without claiming they can never score.
