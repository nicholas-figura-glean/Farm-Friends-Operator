Why these mechanics exist The game as an epistemic time capsule
  • This game is designed to keep scaling. The real farm currently supports output increasing with herd size over the observed range; it does not prove unlimited scaling.
  • Wheat grows here. A historical real-server regime held crops at 0% grown; that negative result remains scoped and overdue for revalidation.
  • Ceiling Lift I and II remember how the short collection cohort appeared capped at run 46 before it was later superseded.
  • The economy is deliberately abstract. Prices are inspired by selected real-farm values, not a model of its full economy.
  • Achievements preserve wrong turns. Wool, At Last and Milk, At Last remember the run-50 collection cohort without claiming that species evidence remains universally true.
